# Kestrel Assurance Staff Leave Policy

**Fictitious sample content.** This document was written for the Kestrel Assistant demonstration. Kestrel Assurance is an invented company, and nothing here is the policy of any real organisation or a statement of any law.

Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People and Culture.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all permanent employees of Kestrel Assurance.

1.2 Employees on fixed-term contracts of 6 months or longer earn leave in proportion to the time they work. Fixed-term contracts shorter than 6 months follow the terms in the contract itself.

## 2. Annual leave

2.1 Annual leave entitlement depends on completed years of continuous service on the first day of the leave year (1 January).

| Band | Completed years of service | Annual leave per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Less than 2 years | 15 working days |
| B | 2 to 4 years | 18 working days |
| C | 5 to 9 years | 21 working days |
| D | 10 years or more | 25 working days |

2.2 Annual leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the yearly entitlement.

2.3 Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March, after which they are forfeited. Exception: when a line manager cancelled approved leave for operational reasons, up to 10 days may be carried over with written approval from People and Culture.

2.4 A request for 5 or more consecutive working days of annual leave must be submitted at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. A request for fewer than 5 days must be submitted at least 2 working days before the first day of leave.

## 3. Sick leave

3.1 Employees receive 30 working days of paid sick leave in each 36-month cycle. During the first 6 months of employment, employees earn 1 day of paid sick leave for every 26 days worked.

3.2 A medical certificate is required for an absence of more than 2 consecutive working days, and for any sick leave taken on a Monday or a Friday more than twice in an 8-week period.

## 4. Family responsibility leave

4.1 Employees with at least 4 months of service receive 3 days of paid family responsibility leave per leave year, for the birth or serious illness of their child, or the death of a spouse, partner, parent, child or sibling.

4.2 Unused family responsibility leave does not carry over to the next leave year.

## 5. Parental leave

5.1 An employee who gives birth may take 16 consecutive weeks of parental leave. The first 8 weeks are paid at full salary and the remaining 8 weeks at 50% of salary.

5.2 Any other parent, including an adoptive parent, may take 10 consecutive working days of parental leave at full salary, starting within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

5.3 The paid portions in 5.1 and 5.2 require 12 months of continuous service on the day leave starts. Employees with less service may take the same leave unpaid, and their role is protected.

## 6. Unpaid leave

6.1 Up to 20 working days of unpaid leave may be granted in a leave year, with the approval of both the line manager and People and Culture.

6.2 Unpaid leave is considered only after the employee's available annual leave has been used.
