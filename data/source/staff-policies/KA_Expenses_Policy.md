# Kestrel Assurance Business Expenses Policy

**Fictitious sample content.** This document was written for the Kestrel Assistant demonstration. Kestrel Assurance is an invented company, and nothing here is the policy of any real organisation. The rates below are invented examples, not official rates.

Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy covers costs that employees pay themselves while doing Kestrel Assurance business and then claim back.

## 2. Principles

2.1 An expense is reimbursed only if it was necessary for business, reasonable in amount, and supported by the documents in section 6.

## 3. Submitting a claim

3.1 Claims must be submitted through the expenses system within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.

3.2 Claims older than 90 days are not paid, unless the Finance Manager approves an exception in writing.

## 4. Approval limits

4.1 Each claim is approved according to its total amount.

| Claim total | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to R2 000 | Line manager |
| R2 000.01 to R10 000 | Head of department |
| More than R10 000 | Chief Financial Officer |

4.2 No one may approve their own claim or a claim from someone they report to.

## 5. Travel

5.1 Business travel in a private vehicle is reimbursed at R4.84 per kilometre. Travel between home and the employee's usual office is not business travel.

5.2 Flights are booked in economy class. Business class is allowed only for a single flight of more than 6 hours, with the approval of an executive.

## 6. Receipts

6.1 A tax invoice or a clear photograph of it is required for every item over R100. For an item of R100 or less, a short written note of the date, amount and purpose is enough.

## 7. Meals and accommodation

7.1 When travelling for business, these daily limits apply per person.

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | R150 |
| Lunch | R200 |
| Dinner | R350 |
| Accommodation, per night | R1 800 |

7.2 Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 8. Costs that are never reimbursed

8.1 Traffic fines and parking fines, personal entertainment, and home internet or electricity (see the home-office allowance in the Remote Work and Equipment Policy) are never reimbursed.
