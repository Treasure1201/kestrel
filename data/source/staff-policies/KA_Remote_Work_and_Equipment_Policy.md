# Kestrel Assurance Remote Work and Equipment Policy

**Fictitious sample content.** This document was written for the Kestrel Assistant demonstration. Kestrel Assurance is an invented company, and nothing here is the policy of any real organisation.

Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People and Culture, with IT Services.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy explains when employees may work away from the office, what equipment Kestrel Assurance provides, and the allowance paid towards the cost of working from home.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 Employees may work remotely once they have completed their probation period of 3 months, if their line manager agrees that the role is suitable.

2.2 Eligible employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are team office days, on which everyone is expected in the office.

2.3 Exception: an employee with an approved workplace accommodation may work remotely for more days, including fully remotely, as set out in the accommodation agreement.

## 3. Working hours

3.1 Remote workers keep the same contracted hours as office workers and must be available during core hours, 09:00 to 15:00, Monday to Friday.

## 4. Equipment

4.1 Kestrel Assurance provides the following equipment for work, whether in the office or at home.

| Item | What is provided | Replacement cycle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laptop | One standard-issue laptop | Every 4 years |
| Monitor | One 24-inch monitor, on request | Every 6 years |
| Headset | One noise-cancelling headset | Every 2 years |
| Office chair | A once-off allowance of up to R3 500 | Not replaced |

4.2 Equipment remains the property of Kestrel Assurance and must be returned within 5 working days of the employee's last working day. An unreturned laptop is recovered from the final salary at its book value.

## 5. Home-office allowance

5.1 Employees who work remotely for at least 2 days per week on average receive a home-office allowance of R450 per month, paid monthly in arrears with salary.

5.2 The allowance covers internet, electricity and other running costs. These costs cannot be claimed separately as expenses.

5.3 Employees on probation do not receive the allowance.

## 6. Security

6.1 Laptops must be locked when unattended, and the company VPN must be used on any network other than the office network.

6.2 A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.

## 7. Working from another country

7.1 Employees may work from outside South Africa for up to 20 working days in a calendar year. The request must be approved at least 15 working days before travel.

7.2 Working from another country is not permitted in roles that handle members' personal information.
