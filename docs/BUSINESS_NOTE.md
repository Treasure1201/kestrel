# Kestrel product assistant: a note for managers

## What it does

Agents ask a question about group life, disability or funeral cover. The assistant searches only the approved Kestrel guides and gives a short answer an agent can read out, with the exact passage or table row it relied on and where to find it. It can look up benefit tables and work out simple amounts, showing the rule it used.

## What it does not do

- It makes no decisions about a specific member or claim. "Will this claim be paid?" is referred to the usual process.
- It knows nothing outside the approved guides. If they do not cover a question, it says so rather than guessing.
- It can miss a condition, such as "the lower of" or "working days". Agents should read the quoted passage before giving a caller an amount, deadline or eligibility rule.

## How much to trust it

Every answer shows its source passage, and the system checks that the quote really is in a current, approved guide and that any numbers add up. If the checks fail, the answer is held back and the agent is pointed to the sections to read. On the 15 test questions, run three times, it answered all 13 that the documents cover correctly each time and correctly said "the documents do not say" for the other 2.

The checks prove the evidence is real, not that the answer reads it correctly, so people still review a sample. ID and phone numbers are removed from questions, but not every name or health detail will be caught: agents should leave member details out.

## What we need from your team

- **Named content owners** for each guide, who approve it and fix or withdraw it quickly.
- **A process for approving new versions** of a guide before agents rely on them.
- **Real questions from agents**, with member details removed, and agreed answers to test against.
- **Reviewers** for flagged and sampled answers each week.
- **Agreed rules on personal information**, set with the Information Officer before any pilot.
